Services

Everything under one roof

Lab, digital X-ray, ultrasound and surgery in the clinic — so diagnosis and treatment happen in the same visit.

  • Surgery & orthopedics

    Spay/neuter, tumour removal, foreign bodies, fracture repair — under monitored gas anaesthesia.

  • Vaccines & prevention

    Puppy and kitten series, annual boosters, rabies and microchip (mandatory in Israel), parasite control.

  • In-house laboratory

    Blood count, biochemistry and urinalysis with results in minutes, during your visit.

  • Digital X-ray & ultrasound

    Immediate imaging for fractures, foreign bodies, chest and abdomen.

  • Dental care

    Scaling under monitored anaesthesia, extractions, gum disease.

  • Internal medicine

    Digestive, skin and allergy, ears and eyes, kidney, diabetes, neurology, senior care.

  • Nutrition & behaviour

    Diet plans, weight loss, medical diets; puppy training, separation anxiety, litter-box problems.

  • Emergencies

    During opening hours come straight in. After hours call the mobile; life-threatening cases go to a 24/7 hospital.

Questions

Do you speak English?

Yes — English-speaking pet owners are welcome. Please tell us when you book so we can take a little extra time.

Do I need an appointment?

It is best to call or WhatsApp first so you don't wait. In an emergency during opening hours, come straight in and call on the way.

Where can I park?

On Brodetsky Street or in the Ramat Aviv Mall car park next door.

I just moved to Tel Aviv. What does my dog need?

A rabies vaccination and municipal registration, a microchip (both mandatory), year-round tick and parasite prevention, and a check-up to review your vaccination records. Bring your pet passport.

What should I do after hours?

Call 052-9709423. If there is no answer and the situation is life-threatening, go to a 24/7 veterinary hospital such as the Hebrew University Veterinary Teaching Hospital in Beit Dagan (03-9688588).
